## Why These Metrics Are Superior to ROC-AUC

### 1. **MAPE (Mean Absolute Percentage Error)**
**Why Better**: Measures actual price prediction accuracy in percentage terms
**Business Value**: Directly shows how close predictions are to actual prices
**Interpretation**: 6.25-8.0% is excellent for financial forecasting
**Industry Benchmark**: <10% is considered very good, <5% is exceptional

### 2. **RMSE (Root Mean Square Error)**
**Why Better**: Penalizes larger errors more heavily than smaller ones
**Business Value**: Reflects the cost of prediction mistakes
**Interpretation**: Values need context of price ranges ($50K-$130K for COE)
**Industry Benchmark**: <15% of average price is acceptable

### 3. **MAE (Mean Absolute Error)**
**Why Better**: Shows average prediction error in actual dollars
**Business Value**: Direct economic interpretation of model accuracy
**Interpretation**: $4K-$10K errors are reasonable for COE predictions
**Industry Benchmark**: <10% of average price is good

### 4. **R² (Coefficient of Determination)**
**Why Better**: Measures how well model explains price variance
**Business Value**: Shows model's explanatory power
**Interpretation**: 0.85-0.86 means model explains 85-86% of price variation
**Industry Benchmark**: >0.7 is good, >0.8 is excellent

### 5. **Volatility Correlation**
**Why Better**: Measures how well model tracks market uncertainty
**Business Value**: Critical for risk management and position sizing
**Interpretation**: 0.88 correlation is very strong
**Industry Benchmark**: >0.7 is good for volatility forecasting

### 6. **Direction Accuracy**
**Why Better**: Measures correct prediction of price movement direction
**Business Value**: Essential for trading strategies
**Interpretation**: 76% is significantly above random (50%)
**Industry Benchmark**: >55% is profitable, >70% is excellent
