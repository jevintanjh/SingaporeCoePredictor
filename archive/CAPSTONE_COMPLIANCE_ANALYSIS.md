**Capstone Requirement**: Create an AI-driven data analytics system for extracting meaningful insights from large datasets to empower data-driven decisions.

**Your Project**: Singapore COE Price Prediction Platform addresses:
- **Real Business Problem**: Singapore's vehicle quota system affects millions of residents and businesses
